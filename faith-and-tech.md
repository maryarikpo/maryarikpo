# Faith and Technology

Elder David A. Bednar teaches that strands woven together make one strong rope. For me, my faith and my work in IT are not separate strands. They belong together.

## My three guiding principles

### 1. Service
My skills are gifts meant to bless others. IT is a service profession, whether I am supporting users, protecting data, or fixing a system.
- Mosiah 2:17
- Matthew 25:40

### 2. Faith and trust in the Lord
Technology changes fast, and I will never be finished learning. I learn by study and by faith, one step at a time.
- Proverbs 3:5–6
- Doctrine and Covenants 88:118
- 2 Nephi 28:30

### 3. Integrity
Being trusted with data is a responsibility. I want to do careful, honest work, even when no one is watching.
- Luke 12:48
- Doctrine and Covenants 58:27–28

## Ethics in IT: every record is a real person

While researching the 2024 Change Healthcare ransomware attack, I learned that one missing safeguard (multifactor authentication) exposed the private health information of millions of people. With my health background, I know those records are real people's diagnoses, bills, and worries.

This changed how I think about security. I want to be the person who speaks up about a small gap before it becomes a big problem.

## AI and health data

Elder Gerrit W. Gong's counsel on artificial intelligence gives me three guideposts: rely on the Spirit, practice wisdom, and choose trusted sources. In health data, this means checking AI results against reliable data, protecting privacy, watching for bias, and using AI to support my thinking, not replace it.

> "You are not a data point. You're a beloved child of God." (Elder Gerrit W. Gong)
