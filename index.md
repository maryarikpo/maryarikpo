# Behind every record is a real person.

Hi, I'm **Mary Andrew Arikpo**, an Information Technology student (System Administration emphasis) at Ensign College, joining from Nigeria.

My path into IT started in health. I earned a degree in Applied Health from BYU–Idaho, then spent over three years supporting students remotely for a US-based team at Springboard, where I kept a 100% satisfaction score for 7+ months in a row. Along the way, I noticed how much people's health and success depend on good data that is handled with care.

Now I'm bringing those two worlds together. I'm building skills in **Linux, cloud servers, and SQL databases**, and my goal is to become a **health data analyst and, later, an epidemiologist**, using technology to help communities stay healthy.

## What I bring

- **Technical skills:** Linux (Ubuntu) on AWS, SQL in SSMS, Excel data tools
- **People skills:** clear, patient communication with users across many countries
- **A health background:** I understand the people behind the data
- **Integrity:** 98%+ accuracy and careful handling of private information
