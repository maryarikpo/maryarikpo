# Welcome, I'm Mary

I'm an Information Technology student (System Administration emphasis) at Ensign College, joining from Nigeria. Before IT, I earned a degree in Applied Health from BYU–Idaho and spent several years supporting students remotely for a US-based team at Springboard.

I chose IT because I want to understand how health and technology work together. My goal is to become a **health data analyst or epidemiologist**, using technology to help people and communities stay healthy.
